# MONEY SHOT audio lab placeholders

These two original synthesized WAV files are generated by
`python3 tools/generate_audio_lab.py` using only Python's standard library.
They contain no recordings, sampled material, or third-party compositions.

- `tonic_loop.wav`: two bars at 120 BPM, mono 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz.
- `finish_overlay.wav`: one bar of rising notes resolving to the loop's tonic,
  followed by a 0.75-second decay.

The lab uses Stream playback and a continuously playing base loop. The
overlay is sought to the loop's bar phase so its resolution meets a bar
boundary without changing the base track's pitch or position during gameplay
slowdown. All musical and cinematic values are experimental lab settings.
These files do not establish the game's music style or production assets.
